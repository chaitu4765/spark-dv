## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do SPARK-DV products differ from traditional SDF?

Our NanoSilver Fluoride technology is designed to be non-staining compared to traditional SDF, while supporting antibacterial and remineralization benefits.

### Are the products safe for pediatric patients?

Yes, our formulations are designed with pediatric safety in mind. Always follow recommended protocols and age guidelines.

### How can I become a distributor?

Contact our partnerships team through the Contact page or use the Distributor Enquiry form.

### Is clinical training available?

Yes — we offer protocol guidance and clinical support. Contact our team to learn more.

### Which products are professional-use only?

P-Obtura Bio-Activ, Nanohybrid Biocem, TRICURE, and MARC 3D Crowns are intended for professional use by qualified dental practitioners.
