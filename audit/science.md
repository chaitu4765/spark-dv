## How Our Technology Works

Understanding the mechanisms behind NanoSilver Fluoride's effectiveness in caries prevention.

### Antibacterial Action

NanoSilver particles (10-20nm) disrupt bacterial cell membranes through multiple mechanisms, reducing the population of acid-producing bacteria that cause caries.

Silver nanoparticles interact with bacterial cell walls, causing structural damage and interfering with respiratory enzymes.

![NSF Antibacterial Mechanism Diagram](https://sparkdv.com/assets/nsf-antibacterial-diagram-Dp4Pyfpy.png)

### Biofilm Disruption

Our nano-sized particles penetrate the extracellular matrix of dental biofilm, disrupting its protective structure.

This allows better penetration of antimicrobial and remineralizing agents to the tooth surface.

![NSF Biofilm Disruption Mechanism Diagram](https://sparkdv.com/assets/nsf-biofilm-diagram-LxLNoosV.png)

### Remineralization Support

Fluoride ions and nano-hydroxyapatite work synergistically to deposit minerals into demineralized enamel.

Nano-hydroxyapatite provides a reservoir of calcium and phosphate ions that support natural remineralization processes.

![NSF Remineralization Mechanism Diagram](https://sparkdv.com/assets/nsf-remineralization-diagram-wFf-cVns.png)

## Safety & Non-Staining Technology

One of the key advantages of our NanoSilver Fluoride technology over traditional silver diamine fluoride (SDF) is our formulation designed to minimize staining.

Traditional SDF treatments often result in black staining of arrested carious lesions, which can be cosmetically unacceptable for many patients. Our patented formulation addresses this concern through stabilized nanosilver particles and optimized delivery systems.

#### Clinical Note

While our products are formulated to be non-staining compared to in-office SDF treatments, individual results may vary. Consult with a dental professional for personalized recommendations.
