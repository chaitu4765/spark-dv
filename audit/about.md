Our Founder

## Dr. Ajay Reddy Mareddy

Founder and Managing Director of SPARK-DV, where science, clinical insight, and product innovation come together to redefine preventive dentistry.

A practicing dentist and academic researcher, he has spent years working at the intersection of patient care, dental biomaterials, and minimally invasive treatment approaches.

His innovation journey is reflected in publicly available WIPO patent publications, including WO2009098710A1 (Formocresol gel) and WO2015155629A1 (Saturation method of a drug for dental). These filings focused on improving dental materials and delivery systems through safer formulations, better clinical handling, and biomaterial-based approaches such as PLGA-assisted obturation and controlled therapeutic release.

Alongside this patent work, Dr. Mareddy has continued to publish research in pediatric and preventive dentistry, including recent PubMed-indexed studies on nanosilver fluoride, silver diamine fluoride, dental sealants, and remineralization-focused care. SPARK-DV builds on that foundation: products shaped not by marketing first, but by decades of clinical observation, formulation research, and evidence-led development.
