1️⃣ Chairside Initiation Protocol (Dentist-Led)

Indications

  • High caries risk patients
  • White spot lesions (non-cavitated)
  • Orthodontic patients
  • Recurrent caries around restorations
  • Xerostomia / reduced salivary flow

Step 1: Baseline Assessment

  • • Record caries risk profile
  • • Identify white spot lesions (ICDAS 1–2)
  • • Document plaque score
  • • Photograph for monitoring

Step 2: Professional Cleaning

  • • Perform prophylaxis
  • • Remove plaque and biofilm
  • • Isolate and dry teeth gently

Step 3: Demonstration

  • • Apply pea-sized amount on dry brush
  • • Demonstrate 2-minute brushing focusing on:
  • → Cervical margins
  • → Interproximal areas
  • → Around brackets (if present)

Step 4: No-Rinse Instruction

  • • Instruct patient to spit, do not rinse
  • • Avoid eating/drinking for 30 minutes

Step 5: Product Selection

  • • Nanion Lite → moderate risk
  • • Nanion Elite → high risk / active demineralization

2️⃣ Home-Use Guidance (Patient Instructions)

Use twice daily
Pea-sized amount
Brush 2 full minutes
Spit, do not rinse
Avoid food/drinks 30 minutes
Children 6+ only, supervised

Follow-Up

  • • Review at 4–6 weeks
  • • Reassess white spots
  • • Adjust risk category if required

Clinical Disclaimer

These protocols are intended for licensed dental professionals. Product usage should follow the official Instructions for Use (IFU). Clinical judgment must guide treatment decisions.
